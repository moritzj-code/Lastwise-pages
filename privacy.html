Lastwise Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Overview

Lastwise is designed to be a simple household reminder app. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or third-party tracking.

Information Stored by the App

Lastwise stores the items you create, completion history, reminder settings, app preferences, and onboarding choices on your device. If you use Apple device backup or iCloud features, Apple may back up that device data according to your Apple settings.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Lastwise schedules local notifications on your device. These reminders are handled by iOS and are used only to remind you about items you chose.

Purchases

Lastwise Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Lastwise does not receive or store your payment card details.

Data Sharing

Lastwise does not sell your personal data. Lastwise does not use your app content for advertising.
