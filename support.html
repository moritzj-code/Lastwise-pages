Common Questions

Where do reminders appear?

Reminder notifications appear through iOS, depending on your notification settings. They may show on the Lock Screen, Notification Center, banners, or Apple Watch if configured.

How do I add the widget?

On the iPhone Home Screen, touch and hold an empty area, choose Edit, select Add Widget, then search for Lastwise and add the medium widget.

How do I manage subscriptions?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID settings. You can also use the restore purchases option inside Lastwise.
